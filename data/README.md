# data

Your private files. Nothing in here is committed except this note and the
examples. `jobsearch init` fills the folder in; you can also copy the
examples by hand.

- `cv.txt` your CV as plain text. The only source of facts the drafts may use.
- `cv.pdf` the PDF attached when a letter is sent by email. Name it in profile.json.
- `profile.json` plain facts for forms, and your situation for the drafts.
- `voice.md` what is worth telling about you, and one approved sample letter.
- `board.json` the board, written by the scan and the page. Not with Redis.
- `notify.json` the ntfy topic. Created on the first ping.
