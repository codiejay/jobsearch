# Voice

Everything under "Worth telling" and "Sample letter" is the only thing
that makes the letters sound like you. Fill both in. The sample should be
a letter you wrote yourself, or one the tool drafted that you fixed until
you would send it. Keep it under 200 words.

Hi Example team,

Happy to talk whenever suits you. Thanks for reading.
